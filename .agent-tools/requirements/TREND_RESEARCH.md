# Fresh Store research and connected screenshot design

Before every release, the agent checks current Apple requirements, the actual listing and current storefront/category trends. For new or materially changed marketing assets, create a dated research note within 30 days of the proposed submission and refresh volatile facts before upload. Reuse a recent valid note for an unchanged hotfix, recording what was rechecked. Do not force a new campaign or delay an urgent fix simply to redraw artwork; exceptions to localization/readiness gates still require the owner.

## Sources and honest metrics

| Need | Useful source | What it establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current free/paid popularity | Apple's public storefront/device/category charts | Rank at capture time, not revenue, profit, keyword demand or a whole month's average |
| Commercial scale/monthly estimates | Accessible Sensor Tower, Appfigures or AppMagic reports/tools; public company filings where relevant | Defined estimated consumer spend/revenue with period/platform/territory; store revenue and company revenue are different |
| Keyword ideas/demand/competition | Authorized Apple Ads keyword tools or accessible AppTweak/Appfigures/Sensor Tower ASO tools | Provider-specific popularity/volume estimates; record methodology/date and do not equate scores across providers |
| Real creative execution | The app's current Apple listing and actual native screenshot/preview assets | The observed design, not proof that it caused downloads or revenue |
| Our own results | App Store Connect analytics/PPO/CPP and eligible attribution | Defined metrics for our app; keep private account data out of public research logs |

Use existing authorized access and free public sources first. Do not buy a subscription, start ads, bypass login/paywalls, scrape against terms, redistribute licensed datasets or copy competitor artwork. AppMagic/Appfigures pages may need an account or may not render through simple fetches: say unavailable rather than invent the values. A few apps do not need a new hosted database: keep concise dated Markdown/CSV evidence in the shared research folder, with source URLs and no copied assets/private analytics. A local SQLite cache is optional only if real volume justifies it.

For every metric capture the app ID, source/retrieval date, storefront, platform, category, window, metric/definition, value or unknown, estimate status and access limits. Distinguish a completed prior month from a partial current-month/daily snapshot. Never call a free-chart entry "top grossing" or call estimated consumer spend profit. Do not label an app financially successful without an appropriate dated source; popularity and awards can be separate reference signals.

## Reference set and creative decisions

Use at least two direct/adjacent references and at least three references across other relevant categories, such as creativity, wellness, finance, travel, productivity/AI and games. Include a credible commercial-scale reference when evidence is accessible; otherwise record that limitation and use verified popularity/reference signals. Choose transferable techniques, not a fixed stale list of winners. Validate each current listing visually, including a few consecutive cards; do not judge from search snippets alone.

Record the first-three-card story, type scale, contrast, native UI prominence, framing, background continuity, illustrations/3D/motion, localized/RTL handling, and what could work for our actual product. Explain what not to copy: awards, financial guarantees, monetization dark patterns, fake gameplay, third-party media or a competitor's signature artwork. High grossing does not make a pattern ethical or appropriate for a free local-first app.

Keyword research is a per-locale hypothesis: seed the verified user problem, collect related terms, annotate relevance/source/volume/competition as measured or unknown, remove misleading/trademark terms, deduplicate against name/subtitle/category and check field limits. Public search position or autocomplete alone does not establish volume. Choose an experiment and baseline rather than promising rankings. Provider model changes can break comparability; record them.

## Panoramic / continuous screenshot campaigns

A continuous curve, ribbon, illustrated world or scene spanning cards is encouraged when it improves the story. Build one editable master canvas per platform/size/locale, with integer-width export slices and a consistent coordinate system. Record the slice order/names/dimensions and preview the full strip plus individual exports. App Store cards have gaps, cropping and variable visible counts: continuity is decorative, not required for understanding.

Each card remains a useful standalone advertisement with a complete short benefit and genuine app-in-use evidence. Do not split essential words, instructions, legal disclosures or a critical interaction across a seam. Keep native UI large enough to read; a ribbon must not obscure it. Check the first 1–3 cards at real phone/Store thumbnail size, adjacent seams, narrow/landscape layouts and each actual device group. Do not rely on a two-card panorama as the only way to understand a feature.

Adapt and inspect localized text, longer strings and RTL gallery behavior; do not blindly reverse the whole strip or mirror app screenshots. Design Mac/iPad campaigns from their real layouts. Keep the current native icon and app accent; a continuous campaign may express the app's own identity rather than force every product into the same scene.

Agents may use the Mac's compatible local Blender/Metal tools for original 3D/lighting, Hyperframes for motion, approved image creation/editing tools and native/vector workflows. Install/configure relevant official tools when needed, retain editable source and rights records, and render/inspect. Use genuine native captures for screenshot UI; local 3D is not permission to invent screens or convert a 2D app/game runtime. No stock-media-only or placeholder shortcut when the task requires finished original craft.

## Research note and release gate

Record: date/window/market/platform; reference app IDs/links; financial vs popularity evidence and limitations; actual consecutive assets inspected; transferable ideas; panoramic storyboard/slices; locale-specific keyword candidates and sources; chosen hypothesis/control; current Apple specification checks; rejected ideas; local tools/render evidence and next measurement date. Link this note from the release packet. Missing research, copied claims, an unreadable panorama, incomplete language coverage or unverified feature promises must be reported before publishing, not silently waived.
