# Voice and copy

Use calm, direct language and sentence case. Name an action with a verb: Save article, Open book, Connect Goodreads. State errors plainly and offer a useful next action. Use “Could not connect. Check the address and try again.” rather than unexplained error codes.

Shared navigation terms: Library, Search, Settings, About, Connections. Keep task-specific language: read. uses reading and highlights; sovo. uses imported audiobooks, playback and listening progress; the separate listen. desktop catalogue uses discovery and client integrations; Immersed. uses subtitles, vocabulary, and immersion.

Product descriptions:
- read. — Books, articles, and highlights.
- listen. desktop catalogue — Find your next audiobook.
- For sovo., describe verified local-player features; do not reuse the catalogue’s discovery/download promises.
- Immersed. — Japanese worth watching.
- Kotoba Atlas — Japanese words, phrases, and context.

Do not describe discovery or download handoff as audiobook playback. Do not claim sync, offline support, privacy properties, or platform availability beyond what the app actually implements.

Use “Open read.”, “Open sovo.”, and “Open Immersed.” for companion actions. Offer a website link when the companion app is not installed. A shared suite name does not imply a shared account or automatic data sharing.

Use “Open listen.” only for links to the separate community desktop catalogue. Internal identifiers such as `listen-mark.svg` do not determine the public Store name.
