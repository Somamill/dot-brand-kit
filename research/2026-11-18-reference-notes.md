# Store reference refresh — 18 November 2026

Scope: US iPhone visual-reference snapshot, with separate recent global commercial reports. This is not a complete November revenue ranking, a keyword-volume study or evidence of conversion lift. No paid data account was used, no competitor media is committed, and no revenue is inferred from appearance.

Apple's US public charts were checked: categories include creativity/video, AI/productivity, shopping and games. They are current popularity snapshots, not profit data. Sensor Tower's Q3 2026 market report identifies ChatGPT as a global grossing leader for that quarter; its October 2026 games report identifies Roblox as the global leading mobile game by estimated consumer spending. These reports have their own platform/territory definitions and do not establish a current-month iOS-only profit ranking. See TREND_RESEARCH.md for the source links.

The first two Apple listing screenshots were downloaded as temporary research files for Canva, Headspace, Revolut, Roblox and Flighty. Both consecutive screenshots for all five were visually inspected. Headspace uses related curved color fields and Flighty repeats its map treatment; this does not establish that either pair was authored as an exact seamless strip. These are cross-category visual references, not unverified revenue winners:

| Reference | Category / observed presentation | Useful lesson / boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Canva](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id897446215) | Creativity: colorful composition grid, then a large editor view | Feature variety can be staged across cards; keep our own clear first action and no copied templates |
